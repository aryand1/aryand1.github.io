Selected Projects

2025–present · DoD-funded (~$2.4M) · social media + public health

SPAR-KG

Project manager and researcher. SPAR-KG (Strategic Prevention for Reduction of Drug Use using Knowledge Graphs) is a multi-investigator project, and I coordinate its technical work across computer science, engineering, and domain teams. The system turns social media into a real-time public-health monitor for emerging drug trends, with a focus on substance-use detection, education, intervention, and trend prediction. It pulls posts, comments, and videos from Reddit, TikTok, and YouTube, then reads them with transformer models, OCR, and speech recognition to catch both explicit and subtle signals. Captions, transcripts, hashtags, and on-screen text merge into single records. A risk-scoring module flags high-risk or viral content, behavioral ontologies interpret substance mentions and psychological cues, and an interactive dashboard shows trends from daily spikes to long-range patterns.

2024–2026 · NSF + ISS National Lab (~$400K) · computer vision

Drop to Data (D2D)

Lead researcher and sole developer. D2D studies how water and ice droplets form and behave in normal and microgravity conditions, where nucleation is hard to observe by traditional means. A YOLOv11 model trained on more than 1,300 hand-annotated microscopy images detects and measures droplets (mAP@0.50 of 0.78), and the pipeline quantifies droplet growth, coalescence, and freezing. A knowledge graph links the observed behavior to the underlying physics. D2D ships as a cloud-based, open-source module that accepts both image and video input, and it replaces a slow manual process with scalable analysis for aerospace and heat-transfer research. The work is published in Frontiers in Thermal Engineering (2026), and a U.S. provisional patent was filed in April 2026.

2024–present · PhD research · ESWC 2026 workshop

PERSEUS

PERSEUS (Perceptual Semantic Extraction & Unified System) builds knowledge graphs and ontologies from text, image, and audio inputs and catches the hallucinations that large language models introduce. An LLM extracts candidate triples, retrieval over hybrid sparse and dense indexes gathers supporting evidence, and a natural language inference step checks each triple against that evidence. Every accepted triple keeps its provenance, and an interactive review stage lets people inspect, refine, and approve the graph. PERSEUS improved CaRB precision by 11 percentage points and cut false positives by 54.6%. It runs fully on locally hosted open-weight models (Llama-3-8B-Instruct via Hugging Face Transformers).

2026–present · ontology search + recommendation

KgReRanker ↗

Sole researcher and developer. Designed and implemented a quality-aware ontology search and recommendation system that retrieves topic-relevant BioPortal ontologies and re-ranks them using semantic relevance and ontology-quality evidence. KgReRanker combines keyword-match signals with four quality dimensions (descriptiveness, definition quality, semantic connectivity, and hierarchical structure), so users can compare candidate ontologies by topical fit and structural quality when they select resources for reuse.

ontology evaluation · IEEE ICSC 2026 · WiseOWL 2.0 accepted in IJSC

WiseOWL

An ontology validation tool that scores any ontology or knowledge graph across four metrics: entity-description quality, semantic connectivity between descriptions and entities, connection richness, and the depth and balance of the class hierarchy. It reports each score plus an average, and automatically flags any metric below 40%. Where it finds gaps, it suggests concrete next steps, such as clarifying descriptions, adding relational links, or rebalancing the hierarchy, so a raw validation result becomes a clear plan for improving reuse.

2023–2024 · co-developer · LLM + Neo4j · Applied Ontology 2025

OLIVE ↗

A semi-automated system that makes ontology building faster and easier to manage. Instead of experts defining every relationship by hand, OLIVE pairs large language models (ChatGPT and LLaMA) with the Neo4j graph database to generate and organize relationships from structured and unstructured data. People enter concepts or short prompts, and OLIVE assembles a knowledge graph showing how they connect, visualized live through WebVOWL. Experts review and correct results in the same interface. Validation with Protégé's reasoner and the OOPS! pitfall scanner confirmed logically sound output, and the workflow cut the time to build an ontology while improving how precisely relationships are captured.

2025 · computer vision + knowledge graphs · K-CAP 2025

GLIIDE

Co-lead researcher (equal contribution). GLIIDE (Global-Local Image Integration via Descriptive Extraction) generates scene graphs from images with two tracks. A YOLOv8 branch detects objects and their attributes, and a DETR-guided BLIP-2 branch writes captions. Llama-3 8B extracts triples from both outputs and fuses them into OWL-formatted knowledge graphs. Published at the 13th International Conference on Knowledge Capture (K-CAP 2025) and invited for an extended journal version in MDPI Computers.

2023 · precision agriculture · CNH Industrial collaboration

Boom Displacement Quantification

Lead researcher. A monocular computer-vision system measures how far an agricultural spray boom moves in the field, using a custom 3D target and a fine-tuned YOLO pipeline. Validated against an inclinometer, it reached mAP@0.50 of 0.995 and a mean absolute error of 0.026 m. Better boom control helps sprayers apply chemicals more accurately. Published in Computers and Electronics in Agriculture (2026) and featured on Kansas Public Radio's Research Matters.

2024 · evidence-checked ontology learning · Open Conference Proceedings 2026

Echo-LLM

Developed evidence-checked, LLM-supported ontology learning methods for more reliable hierarchical knowledge construction, combining generated concepts with verification before incorporation into the ontology.

Nurture KG
